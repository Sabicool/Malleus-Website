Register as a member

Join Malleus as a general member to receive project updates, gain voting rights at AGMs, and be eligible to nominate for Executive and General Committee positions.

What happens after you register? Your name and email are added to a secure database used only to send community updates. You'll get voting rights at AGMs and can attend the fortnightly General Committee meetings. There are no formal responsibilities — just community membership. Want to become a Maintainer? Email it@malleus.org.au to request a dedicated @malleus.org.au address (these are no longer created automatically). You can opt out and delete your data at any time by emailing admin@malleus.org.au.
